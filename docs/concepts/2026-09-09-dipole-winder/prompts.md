# Image-generation prompts

Tool: built-in `image_gen.imagegen`. Two independent initial generations, followed by one targeted connector-detail correction for each board. Final assets are concept mockups rather than dimensional CAD.

## A — initial generation

Use case: product-mockup. Create a thoughtful industrial design concept board for a 3D printed ultralight QRP dipole antenna center that is also a mirrored two-sided wire winder. This is brainstorming, not a dimensionally certified CAD render. Warm off-white background, restrained dark graphite typography, realistic matte terracotta-orange printed PETG with gentle layer lines, brushed nickel BNC, subtle shadows. Landscape board. Main large three-quarter view of the deployed empty winder body on the left, smaller front-on packed-wire view on the right. Small side-view inset clearly shows the 90-degree shelf.

CRITICAL GEOMETRY AND ORIENTATION: The flat main body lies in an X-Y plane and hangs with its long Y axis vertical. It is a vertically oriented long narrow triangulated open spine with FOUR swept rounded winding horns: an upper-left and lower-left horn define ONE tall narrow winding bay on the left; upper-right and lower-right define a SECOND tall narrow bay on the right. Each pair is like a K6ARK ultralight skeletal wire winder rotated upright; the second is its mirror across the vertical spine. Overall silhouette like a slender open butterfly or double-sided long H, with big open triangular cutouts rather than a solid plate. Do NOT draw reels, circular spools, disks, bobbins or rotating mechanisms. Strong rounded rails and horn tips. One sturdy circular suspension eye at the very top of the central spine, centered over the BNC, with a short olive tree-hanging cord leading upwards. This eye is part of the FLAT body, not a separate hinged part.

In the middle is a short BNC mounting shelf which projects toward the viewer out of the flat body at exactly 90 degrees, braced by two small triangular root gussets. The shelf is horizontal when the unit is hanging. A SINGLE female panel-mount BNC passes through this shelf: its mating barrel points straight DOWN toward the bottom end of the central spine, parallel to the broad main body, NOT straight out toward the viewer. The connector rear solder connections/nut are ABOVE the shelf; the mating barrel is BELOW the shelf. The side-view inset should unambiguously look like an L: vertical main spine, horizontal short shelf, vertical downward BNC barrel. No extra coax connector or transformer.

The packed view shows orange/red insulated antenna wire neatly wrapped many times TOP-TO-BOTTOM around ONLY the left horn pair, and blue-gray insulated wire separately wrapped TOP-TO-BOTTOM around ONLY the right horn pair. Two separate tall narrow wire bundles at x<0 and x>0, BOTH clear of the central BNC and hanging eye; no wire wound across the center, no shared bundle across all four horns. The wire colors only distinguish legs. Coax disconnected in packed view. One discreet tip parking notch on each wing.

The main deployed view has both winding bays EMPTY and shows two thin antenna wires exiting to the left and right from the central strain-relief regions; the BNC has a short dark coax segment plugged below, falling downward along the center. Keep wiring physically plausible and separated. Clear small typography and delicate leader lines, no decorative badges, no invented certification, no logos, no RF ratings. Footer must say "Concept only · dimensions and wire capacity to verify".
VARIANT A — Title text exactly "A / DIRECT-SOLDER BUTTERFLY". Subtitle "One frame. Two independent winding bays." Narrow open center spine, compact central feedpoint island. Starting visual proportions about 140 mm high by 95 mm wide, 4 mm thick base; no dimension arrows. There are THREE clearly separated small round radiused strain-relief holes in a row on each side of the center island, six total. Each deployed antenna wire weaves through its own holes, then has a small relaxed insulated service bend to one of the two separate rear BNC solder contacts above the shelf, one center cup and one shell tag. Do NOT make tensioned wires terminate directly at BNC. Do NOT include screw posts, ring terminals or a junction board. Show two tiny protective rails around slack solder tails, leaving assembly accessible. Labels exactly "Tree suspension eye", "Left-leg winding bay", "Right-leg winding bay", "Three-hole strain relief", "Slack tails to BNC", "90° mounting shelf", "PACKED", "SIDE VIEW". Prioritize readable faithful physical topology over excessive detail.
