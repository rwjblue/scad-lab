Walk it out. Pack it together. A portable BNC dipole center with two removable figure-eight winders. Each leg gets its own winder for walking out from the mast; clip the three pieces together for packing.

Revision 3 adds larger retaining lips and matching release clearance, with 4 mm standoffs and a 10.1 mm BNC cutout. This is a prototype: the revised retention and compact fit with wound wire still need physical testing. CAD illustrations shown.

Hardware

  • 1 × Amphenol RF 31-221-RFX BNC connector
  • 2 × M3 × 16 mm screws, 4 M3 nuts, 8 M3 washers; two compact ring terminals matched to your wire
  • Antenna wire, two short insulated jumpers, suspension cord and two short silicone cable ties

Printing

Print center.stl once and winder.stl twice, or print the complete three-piece print_layout.stl. Use a matching revision-3 center and two revision-3 winders. Both winders must use the same standoff height.

Assembly and use

Fit the BNC and terminal posts as described in the BNC Dipole Center instructions. Weave each leg through its three strain-relief holes and leave slack to the posts. Check continuity to the separate legs and no center-to-shell short.

  1. Release: support the tied winders. Pinch one end's upper/lower tabs inward and lift by the fixed outer bridge. Let that end rest on the stud tops, then pinch and lift the other end until level. Lift the center clear; either end can go first.
  2. Deploy: secure the center low at the mast. Open one tie and unwind as you walk that leg out. Repeat, then hoist. Unwind both legs fully before operating.
  3. Pack: wind from each tip toward the center in a figure eight. Tie each coil around the winder's central brace. Align the studs, pinch to seat each end, then release the catches beneath the heads. Keep wire clear of the clips and gently pull-check both ends.

Test retention and one-hand handling with your actual wire and ties, including glove access. If the lowered center presses on a coil or the grip is cramped, increase the standoff. An outer silicone tie can secure the whole bundle.

Customize and learn more

Download all five .scad files into the same folder, then open modular_dipole.scad in OpenSCAD's Customizer. The other four files supply the winding profile and shared geometry.

  • BNC hole size: bnc_hole_d sets the cutout diameter, from 9.7 to 10.2 mm (default 10.1 mm), retaining the anti-rotation flat.
  • Support height: standoff sets the gap above the winder in millimeters (default 4 mm).
  • Winding space: adjust wing_length and winding_span. Strain-relief spacing and the other hole sizes are adjustable too.

The 40 m / 80 m presets only show estimated wire space; they do not change the supplied STLs or guarantee capacity.
