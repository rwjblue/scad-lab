A compact, one-piece protective nose guard for the N0SA RLP V3 dual-lever CW paddle. The closed bumper, roof, sides and continuous floor shield the finger pieces during transport. Two magnets in the guard align with the key's existing front underside pair.

V6 fit-test prototype. This revision corrects the nose depth for the measured 27.11 mm paddle projection. The earlier prototype printed nose-down without supports; physical fit and magnetic retention of this longer version are still being checked. CAD illustrations shown.

Parts needed for one guard

  • 1 printed guard from the file table below.
  • 2 neodymium disc magnets, 6 mm diameter × 2 mm thick, magnetized through the thickness. This is the next test configuration.
  • Alternatively, use 2 discs, 6 mm diameter × 1.5 mm thick, with the matching thin-magnet STL.
  • A small amount of adhesive suitable for your PETG and magnet coating, used according to its instructions.

The key keeps all of its existing magnets. Only two new magnets go into the guard; no screws, clips or key disassembly are required.

Magnet sources: the 6 × 2 mm, 300-piece Amazon option is a dimension-matched example; its grade and retention in this guard are unverified. The 6 × 1.5 mm N48, 100-piece Amazon option is the thinner hardware already sourced for the project. Only two magnets per guard are needed. Verify the selected size on Amazon; product families contain several diameters and thicknesses.

Buy and measure metric 6 mm discs. A true 1/4 inch diameter is 6.35 mm and exceeds the 6.20 mm pockets. N48 is specified for the linked 1.5 mm option; no grade is established for the 2 mm example. Do not use a supplier's ideal pull-force figure as the guard's retention rating.

The matching fit_coupon checks underside alignment using less filament. The optional magnet_gauge checks diameter tolerance: the notched end is the looser pocket. Both are available for each magnet thickness and print floor-down. They are fit aids, not pieces that attach to the finished guard.

Printing

Starting settings: PETG, 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.20 mm layers and three walls. The full guard STLs are already nose-down: closed bumper on the bed, open end facing up. Keep that orientation and start with supports off, following the successful earlier print. Check the sideways pocket ceilings and small stop pads in the preview and on the print.

Build-plate dimensions are approximately 41.67 × 35.07 mm, standing 41.47 mm tall. The floor-down coupon and gauge also start at Z=0. STL files do not contain material or support settings.

Fit and install the magnets

  1. Dry-fit the plastic without magnets. Slide it on gently. Check both paddles through full travel, including the taper, roof and closed nose. The nominal inside tip clearance is about 2 mm. Confirm the small stops bear on stationary body features.
  2. Check magnet seats and alignment. Use the depth-matched gauge/coupon if helpful, then the full guard. Its sideways-printed pockets can fit differently from the floor-down gauge. Do not force an oversized magnet into a pocket.
  3. Match polarity separately for each pocket. Check attraction to the corresponding key magnet and mark the face before gluing. The two guard magnets need not have the same orientation.
  4. Install after printing. Follow the adhesive's preparation and cure instructions. Keep adhesive off the key, paddles and exposed mating surfaces. Fully seated magnets sit about 0.15 mm below the guard's key-facing floor.
  5. Check retention after curing. Confirm smooth fitting/removal, paddle clearance and resistance to gentle sideways/twisting loads. The magnets retain the guard; the shell and stationary stops provide the intended protection. Loose trial magnets can be pulled out by the key.

Compatibility and editable source

Designed around measurements of one N0SA RLP V3. Fit to other RLP versions or the SP4 is not established. This is a front paddle guard; rear-jack mounting and reversible storage are not implemented.

The self-contained n0sa_rlp_v3_guard.scad includes guard, coupon and gauge variants. It defaults to 1.5 mm magnets; set insert_thickness=2.0 for the primary test option. Magnet diameter, pocket clearance, body clearance, nose length and stops are adjustable. Keep the measured magnet centers until physical fit indicates an adjustment is needed.
